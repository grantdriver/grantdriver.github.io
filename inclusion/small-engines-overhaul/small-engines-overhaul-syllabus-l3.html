🌟 About this course

An introductory course for students interested in small gas engines.

You learn the names and uses of tools, engine parts and their jobs, and engine theory.

Later in the semester: troubleshooting, and the tear-down and rebuild of a small gas engine.

🗓️ What we do in class

📖Readings that follow the class lesson, closer to the regular text
📝Guided notes for each lesson
✅Quick checks at the end of a lesson or unit
🔊Read aloud on every notes page and reading
🎮Unit review games
🔧Shop labs and your own engine project, worked on the whole period

📚 Units, in order

Same units and same order as the class. Unit dates follow the class calendar.

🛡️Unit 0
Shop Start-Up & Safety
Shop rules, safety gear, fuel and fire safety, and the shop safety test.
📏Unit 1
Measuring Tools
Rulers, dial calipers, micrometers and feeler gauges, and how to read them.
🧑‍🔧Unit 2
Engine Careers
Jobs that fix engines and machines, training after high school, and shop skills employers want.
⚙️Unit 3
4-Stroke Theory & Engine Parts
The four strokes (intake, compression, power, exhaust), 2-stroke vs 4-stroke, and the main engine parts.
💨Unit 4
Compression
Why an engine needs compression, how rings and valves seal, and the compression test.
⛽Unit 5
Carburetion & Fuel
How the carburetor mixes air and fuel, the venturi, chokes, and fresh fuel and oil.
⚡Unit 6
Ignition
How the magneto and spark plug make the spark, air gap and spark plug gap.
📘Unit 7
Using the Repair Manual
Finding the model number, using the index and charts, specs, parts look-up and warranties.
🔧Unit 8
Engine Teardown & Rebuild
Taking an engine apart and putting it back together, rings and valves, troubleshooting, and outdoor power equipment care.
