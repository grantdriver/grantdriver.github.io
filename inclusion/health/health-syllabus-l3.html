🌟 About this course

Health prepares you for lifelong decision-making and problem-solving about health and wellness.

The main goal: take an active role in building a healthy lifestyle.

Core ideas: Wellness, Nutrition, Substance Use and Abuse, Social-Emotional Health, Sexual Health, and Safety.

🗓️ What we do in class

📖Readings that follow the class lesson, closer to the regular text
📝Guided notes for each lesson
✅Quick checks at the end of a lesson or unit
🔊Read aloud on every notes page and reading
🎮Unit review games
👥Same units, same order and same points as the class

📚 Units, in order

Same units and same order as the class. Unit dates follow the class calendar.

🌈Unit 1
Wellness
The 8 dimensions of wellness, sleep, strengths, coping with stress, the DECIDE model, SMART goals and money choices.
🥗Unit 2
Food & Nutrition
Macronutrients and micronutrients, the Healthy Eating Plate, whole and processed foods, reading a label, added sugar and planning meals.
🧠Unit 3
Substance Use & Abuse
What a substance is, the teen brain, the spectrum of use and stages of change, risk and protective factors, fentanyl and overdose, laws and getting help.
💬Unit 4
Social-Emotional Health
The 5 SEL skills, identity and self-esteem, naming feelings (RULER), a feel-good plan, setting boundaries, ethical decisions, bullying and mental health.
🫶Unit 5A
Sexual Health 1: Bodies, Pregnancy & Relationships
Reproductive systems, fertilization and pregnancy, healthy/unhealthy/abusive relationships, consent, online safety, and gender and sexuality words.
🩺Unit 5B
Sexual Health 2: STIs, Contraception & Safer Sex
Abstinence, common STIs, testing and treatment, how birth control and condoms work, and how to help a friend get tested.
🚑Unit 6
Safety, First Aid & CPR
Preventing injuries, when to call 911, basic first aid, heart attack vs. cardiac arrest, hands-only CPR and the AED, and summer safety.
🌟Unit 7
Hope, Purpose & Wonder
What hope is and where it comes from, goals plus pathways plus willpower, a legacy map, purpose and a look back at the semester.
