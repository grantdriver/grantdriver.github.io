🌟 What is this class?

World History is the story of people around the world.

You learn about places, religions, wars and big changes.

You look for ways people and times are alike.

🗓️ What we do in class

📺Follow the class slides, made easier to read
📝Guided notes with a word bank
🔊Read aloud for notes, readings and quizzes
🃏Vocabulary cards for key words
🎮Review games before quizzes
✅Short quizzes with 3 choices
🎨A project each unit: art, a talk or an activity

📚 Units, in order

🌍Unit 1
Geography & World Religions
Continents, oceans and maps, and five big world religions.
🕌Unit 2
Muslim Empires
The Ottoman, Safavid and Mughal empires, their art and leaders.
🎨Unit 3
The Renaissance
A rebirth of art and ideas in Italy, and the printing press.
🪖Unit 4
World War I
What caused World War I, trench warfare, and the peace treaty.
🚩Unit 5
The Russian Revolution
The end of the tsars, Lenin, and Stalin's rule.
💸Unit 6
Between the Wars
Money troubles, the Great Depression, and the rise of dictators.
✈️Unit 7
World War II
The main events of World War II, from Poland to D-Day and the end.
🕊️Unit 8
Genocide & Human Rights
How hate grows, the Holocaust, and standing up for human rights.
🧱Unit 9
Cold War, China & India
The Cold War, the Berlin Wall, China under Mao, and India's freedom.
⛪Unit 10
The Reformation
Martin Luther and how the church split into new groups.
💡Unit 11
The Enlightenment
New ideas about science, freedom and fair government.
🇫🇷Unit 12
The French Revolution
The French people rise up for rights. Napoleon takes over.
🌎Unit 13
Latin American Revolutions
Haiti, Mexico and South America win freedom.
🏭Unit 14
The Industrial Revolution
Steam machines, factories, big cities and unions.
