🌟 What is this class?

Ag Science is about farms, food and animals.

You learn what Washington farms grow.

You learn about FFA and farm jobs.

🗓️ What we do in class

📺Follow the class slides, made easier to read
📝Guided notes with a word bank
🔊Read aloud for notes, readings and quizzes
🃏Vocabulary cards for key words
🎮Review games before quizzes
🌱Labs and projects with picture task cards

📚 Units, in order

🌾Unit 1
Ag Words & Whatcom Farms
Farm words and what farms near us grow.
🥔Unit 2
Washington Crops: Potatoes, Wheat & Apples
Washington potatoes, wheat and apples, with Potato and Apple labs.
🚜Unit 3
U.S. Food & Farm Facts
How many people one farmer feeds, and farm families.
🌱Unit 4
FFA & Ag Careers
What FFA is, its colors and motto, and ag jobs.
📊Unit 5
Ag Experience Project
Pick a topic, set a goal, keep a log, make slides and share.
🐄Unit 6
Dairy
How milk is made, dairy foods, and making ice cream.
🐖Unit 7
Livestock Judging
Look at sheep, goats, cattle and pigs and pick the best.
🍎Unit 8
Final Review
Review every unit and check your work log.
🌍Unit 9
Ag Issueslater
Coming later.
