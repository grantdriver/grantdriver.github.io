🌟 About this course

World History is a required two-semester course for 9th grade, from the 1300s to the early 20th century.

It mixes a chronological and regional approach, so studies will not always follow a strict timeline.

Goal: find common ties between times and peoples, and become more informed, understanding world citizens.

🗓️ What we do in class

📖Readings that follow the class lesson, closer to the regular text
📝Guided notes for each lesson
✅Quick checks at the end of a lesson or unit
🔊Read aloud on every notes page and reading
🎮Unit review games
👥Same units, same order and same points as the class
🎨A project in each unit (art, presentation or activity) and a review sheet before each unit test

📚 Units, in order

Same units and same order as the class. Unit dates follow the class calendar.

🌍Unit 1
Geography & World Religions
Continents, oceans, the equator and map types. Five big world religions: Hinduism, Judaism, Buddhism, Christianity and Islam (plus Sikhism), with their symbols, holy books and places.
🕌Unit 2
Muslim Empires
Three Muslim empires (about 1300-1700): the Ottomans, the Safavids and the Mughals (Akbar, the Taj Mahal). Cultural blending.
🎨Unit 3
The Renaissance
A 'rebirth' of Greek and Roman ideas that began in Italy (about 1350-1600). Florence and the Medici, humanism, the printing press, and art by Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael.
🪖Unit 4
World War I
1914-1918. The MAIN causes, the spark in Sarajevo, the alliance 'domino effect', trench warfare and new weapons, and the Treaty of Versailles.
🚩Unit 5
The Russian Revolution
1917: the end of the tsars (Nicholas II), Lenin and the Bolsheviks ('Peace, Land and Bread'), Marx's communism, the civil war, and Stalin's totalitarian rule.
💸Unit 6
Between the Wars
1919-1939. Hyperinflation in Germany, the Great Depression, and the rise of dictators: Hitler, Mussolini and military leaders in Japan.
✈️Unit 7
World War II
1939-1945. Germany invades Poland, blitzkrieg, the Battle of Britain, Pearl Harbor, D-Day, the Pacific war, the atomic bombs, and VE and VJ Day.
🕊️Unit 8
Genocide & Human Rights
How hate grows into genocide: the Holocaust, the Armenian Genocide, apartheid in South Africa and Rwanda. Human rights, upstanders and remembering the victims.
🧱Unit 9
Cold War, China & India
After 1945: the Cold War between the USA and the USSR, the Iron Curtain, NATO, the Berlin Wall; Mao and communist China; Gandhi, independence and partition in India.
⛪Unit 10
The Reformation
Martin Luther and the 95 Theses (1517), the printing press, Calvin, Henry VIII and the Church of England, and the Counter-Reformation.
💡Unit 11
The Enlightenment
The Scientific Revolution, Hobbes and Locke, Montesquieu, Voltaire, Rousseau and Wollstonecraft, and how their ideas shaped the American Revolution.
🇫🇷Unit 12
The French Revolution
The three estates, the Bastille and the Rights of Man (1789), the Reign of Terror, and Napoleon.
🌎Unit 13
Latin American Revolutions
Colonial society, the Haitian Revolution, Bolívar and San Martín, and independence in Mexico and Brazil.
🏭Unit 14
The Industrial Revolution
Why it began in Britain, steam and new inventions, factory life and child labor, growing cities, unions and reforms.
