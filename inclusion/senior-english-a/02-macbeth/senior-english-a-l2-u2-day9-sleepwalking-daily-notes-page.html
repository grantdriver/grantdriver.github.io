📜 Lady Macbeth sleepwalks — Shakespeare
Out, damned spot! out, I say!
What, will these handsne’er be clean?
💬 In plain words: The spot won't come off. My hands will never be clean. I still smell the blood.
William Shakespeare, Macbeth, Act V, Scene 1 (Project Gutenberg eBook #1533). Public domain.
damned spot = the stain she imagines · ne'er = never · perfumes of Arabia = the finest perfumes · sweeten = make smell nice
