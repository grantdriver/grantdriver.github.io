🌟 What is this class?

US History is the story of our country.

You learn big events and the people in them.

You see how the past affects your life today.

🗓️ What we do in class

📺Follow the class slides, made easier to read
📝Guided notes with a word bank
🔊Read aloud for notes, readings and quizzes
🃏Vocabulary cards for key words
🎮Review games before quizzes
✅Short quizzes with 3 choices
🎨Small projects: art, research, presentations

📚 Units, in order

🗓️Unit 1
Timeline & Political Cartoons
Put big events in order. Learn to read a political cartoon.
🦬Unit 2
The Heartland: Moving West
Trains, farms and cowboys in the West, and what it did to Native nations.
🗽Unit 3
Immigration & Cities
Why people came to America and life in crowded cities.
🏭Unit 4
Industry & the Progressives
Inventors, big business, child labor and new safety laws.
⚖️Unit 5
Civil Rights at the Turn of the Century
Unfair Jim Crow laws and the leaders who fought them.
🌎Unit 6
Imperialism
Why the US took new lands, and the Panama Canal.
🪖Unit 7
World War I
Why World War I started, why the US joined, and how it ended.
🗳️Unit 8
Women's Suffrage
How women won the right to vote in 1920.
🎷Unit 9
The 1920s & Harlem Renaissance
Jazz, the Harlem Renaissance, and big changes in the 1920s.
🍞Unit 10
The Great Depression & New Deal
The 1929 crash, hard times, the Dust Bowl and the New Deal.
