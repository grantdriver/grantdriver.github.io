🌟 About this course

Transitions builds skills for life after high school: self-advocacy, independent living, health, work, money and getting around.

Each skill is taught step by step: watch, practice together, then try it yourself.

Some Fridays are cooking labs or community outings.

🗓️ What we do in class

☀️Warm-up and Last Time review
📣Direct instruction: one step card per slide, teacher models
🤝Guided practice: card review, partner or stations, then solo tries
🗂️Wrap-up: share-out with the Skill Card and an exit check
📖Level 3 readings, guided notes and quick checks with read aloud
🍳Some Fridays: cooking lab or community outing

📚 Units, in order

🗣️Unit 1
Self-Advocacy
Strengths, learning profile, the IEP, asking for accommodations, assertive phrases, problem solving, rights and boundaries. Project: One-Page Profile and goal board.
🏠Unit 2
Independent Living
Hand dishwashing, wiping and sanitizing tables, sorting utensils, sweeping, laundry sorting and folding. Project: “My Clean-Up Routine” binder.
🧼Unit 3
Health, Wellness & Relationships
Hygiene routines, healthy choices, personal space and boundaries, and reading emotion cues. Project idea: “My Healthy Day.”
💼Unit 4
Employment & Career Exploration
Work soft skills, professional hygiene, career interests and task-card completion. Project idea: “Job Try-Out Passport.”
💵Unit 5
Financial Literacy & Money
Coin and bill ID, price matching, needs vs. wants and cash transactions. Project idea: a class store day.
🚌Unit 6
Community Navigation & Mobility
Pedestrian safety, community signs, walking routes and public etiquette. Project idea: “My Safe Route” map.

🎒 What you need

  • 📒Skill binder (one page per skill)
  • ✏️Pencil
  • 🃏Skill Card for stamps
  • 💻Laptop for read aloud

⭐ How you show what you learned

  • 🙋Daily participation and effort, in the way that works for you.
  • 📒Notebook or binder pages for each lesson.
  • ✅Quick checks and exit lines at the end of class.
  • 🏆A unit project or showcase at the end of each unit.
  • 🎯Progress on your goals, tracked with your teacher.

💛 Classroom expectations

  • 💛Respect partners: wait your turn and give helpful feedback.
  • 🏫Follow safety steps at every station.
  • 🟨Ask for help any time with the “I need help” card.
  • 🧹Reset and clean up before the wrap-up.

🦺 Kitchen & cleaning safety

  • 🧼Hand washing before food and after cleaning.
  • 🧴Clean vs. sanitize: read labels and use cleaners only with staff.
  • 🔥Kitchen safety on cooking-lab days: hot surfaces, knives, clean-up.
  • 🚶Community outings: stay with your group and follow staff directions.

🙋 Getting help

  • 🙋Ask for help early, before a test or due date
  • 🔊Read aloud and printables are on ferndaleinclusion.com
  • 📂If you are absent, get the notes and handouts when you come back
