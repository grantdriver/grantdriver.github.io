🌟 About this course

Social emotional learning (SEL) builds the five CASEL skills: self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship skills and responsible decision-making.

Each unit has a project that grows lesson by lesson.

Units 6 to 11 are working titles and may change.

🗓️ What we do in class

😊Weekly check-in form and a daily energy check
🔁Last Time review: explain or apply, not just name
⭐Main block: short lesson, choice cards, apply it, card sort, review game
📒Project time: one notebook page per session
👋Closing: exit line and next-class preview
📖Level 3 readings, guided notes and quick checks with read aloud

📚 Units, in order

🤝Unit 1
Foundations & Community
Class routines, daily check-ins and belonging: building a safe class community.
🪞Unit 2
Self-Awareness: Who Am I?
Identity, naming emotions and strengths. Project: an Identity Map folder.
🧰Unit 3
Self-Management: My Calm-Down Toolkit
Breathing and grounding tools, kind self-talk, a reset plan and a calm-down goal. Project: toolkit folder or binder.
🕵️Unit 4
Social Awareness: Feelings Detective
Reading face, body and voice clues, perspective taking, empathy (showing I care) and sympathy (ways to help). Project: Reading Others Notebook.
💬Unit 5
Relationship Skills
What makes a good friend, starting a conversation, active listening, taking turns, kind words, joining in and including others. Ends with a Conversation Showcase.
🛑Unit 6
Stop, Think, Choose
Responsible decision-making: a choice routine, consequences and safety. (Working title.)
🌱Unit 7
I Can Grow
Interests, values and growth mindset: my growth story, then, now and next. (Working title.)
🎯Unit 8
My Game Plan: Goals & Routines
Goal setting, planning and routines with a two-week tracker. (Working title.)
👀Unit 9
Reading the Room
Social awareness in different settings: norms, strengths in others and gratitude. (Working title.)
🧩Unit 10
Team Up & Work It Out
Teamwork, fair conflict fixes and peer pressure. (Working title.)
🏆Unit 11
My SEL Showcase
Year in review: one piece for each CASEL area in a portfolio showcase. (Working title.)

🎒 What you need

  • 📁Folder for handouts
  • 📒Unit notebook (it stays in class)
  • ✏️Pencil
  • 💻Laptop for read aloud and check-in forms

⭐ How you show what you learned

  • 🙋Daily participation and effort, in the way that works for you.
  • 📒Notebook or binder pages for each lesson.
  • ✅Quick checks and exit lines at the end of class.
  • 🏆A unit project or showcase at the end of each unit.
  • 🎯Progress on your goals, tracked with your teacher.

💛 Classroom expectations

  • 💛Respect: what is shared in class stays kind.
  • 🏫Safety: use calm-down tools when you need them.
  • 👂Listen and take turns when others share.
  • 🙋You may always pass on sharing or ask for a break.

🙋 Getting help

  • 🙋Ask for help early, before a test or due date
  • 🔊Read aloud and printables are on ferndaleinclusion.com
  • 📂If you are absent, get the notes and handouts when you come back
