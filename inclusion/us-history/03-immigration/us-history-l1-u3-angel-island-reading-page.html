Angel Island: The Immigration Station in the West

🏝️ Angel Island is an island in San Francisco Bay, California.

🚢 From 1910 to 1940, many immigrants stopped there first. Most came from China.

🚫 A U.S. law, the Chinese Exclusion Act, kept most Chinese workers out.

❓ Officers asked hard questions. How many windows are in your house? Who lives next door?

⏳ Some people waited weeks or months. A few waited two years.

✍️ Some carved poems into the wooden walls. The poems tell about sadness and hope.

🏛️ Today Angel Island is a park and museum.
