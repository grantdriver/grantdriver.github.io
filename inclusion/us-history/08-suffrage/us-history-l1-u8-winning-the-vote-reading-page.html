Winning the Vote

🗳️ Long ago, women in the U.S. could not vote.

📜 In 1848 women met at Seneca Falls, New York. They said women should have equal rights.

🚓 Susan B. Anthony voted in 1872. She was arrested, but she kept fighting.

📣 Alice Paul led a big parade. Women stood at the White House with signs.

🏭 During World War I, women did many jobs. People saw women help the country.

🎉 In 1920, the 19th Amendment gave women the right to vote.
