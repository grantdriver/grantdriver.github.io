Goes with the class Canvas module: “Unit 1 Lightning: L1.1 Initial Model, L1.2 Smaller Scales, L1.3 Sparks, L1.5 Particle Model of Static, L1.6 Cause of Static”.

Goes with the class Canvas module: “Unit 1 Lightning: L1.7 Coulomb's Law, L1.8 Neutral Interactions, L1.9 Static Interaction Review”.
