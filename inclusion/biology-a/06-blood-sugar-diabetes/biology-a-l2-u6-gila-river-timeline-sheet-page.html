This case study is about the Akimel O’otham (also called Pima by Spanish newcomers) of the Gila River.

Use only the class cards and reading. Be respectful.

Arrange all the cards, then write the dates in order and a short label for each.

1. How did losing river water change life for people along the Gila River?

2. The class reading links a change in diet after famine (commodity foods) with later high rates

of obesity and diabetes. In one sentence, what changed?

3. The community is described as resilient. What is one later change that helped (water, work,

or farms)?
