🌍 Unit 1: Geography & World Religions

🕌 Unit 2: Muslim Empires

Three Muslim empires (about 1300-1700): the Ottomans (Constantinople, Suleiman, janissaries), the Safavids (Persia, art and rugs) and the Mughals (India, Akbar, the Taj Mahal). Cultural blending.
Goes with the class Canvas module: “Unit 2 Muslim Empires: pre-quiz, Ottoman Summary, Ottoman Leaders chart (Suleiman DBQ, janissaries), Ottomans & Safavids easy read, Safavid Summary, PBS Safavid + Khan Academy Safavid Art Search, Persian rug sketch, Cultural Blending worksheet, Mughal reading, Secrets of the Taj Mahal video sheet, Muslim Empires Test”.

🎨 Unit 3: The Renaissance

A 'rebirth' of Greek and Roman ideas that began in Italy (about 1350-1600). Florence and the Medici, humanism, the printing press, and art by Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael (perspective, fresco, sfumato).
Goes with the class Canvas module: “Unit 3 Renaissance (current, starts 11/3): pre-quiz, Italy Summary / Cradle of the Renaissance reading, Medieval vs. Renaissance art note sheet (2018Renaissanceart slides), Crash Course World History: The Renaissance video sheet, Ted Ed video + bookwork, School of Athens (Khan Academy), Leonardo & Michelangelo chart, Renaissance Review Hunt slides, quiz”.

🪖 Unit 4: World War I

1914-1918. The MAIN causes (militarism, alliances, imperialism, nationalism), the spark in Sarajevo, the alliance 'domino effect', trench warfare and new weapons, and the Treaty of Versailles.
Goes with the class Canvas module: “Unit 4 World War I: Causes of WWI video sheet (MAIN causes, the spark, domino effect), WWI weapons slides (new technology, stalemate), Sgt Stubby videos, Edgenuity WWI lessons, Treaty of Versailles / Wilson's 14 Points sheet, Kahoot”.

🚩 Unit 5: The Russian Revolution

1917: the end of the tsars (Nicholas II), Lenin and the Bolsheviks ('Peace, Land and Bread'), Marx's communism, the civil war (Red vs. White Army), and Stalin's totalitarian rule.
Goes with the class Canvas module: “Unit 5 Revolutions in Russia: Marx & Lenin compare (Communist Manifesto, Venn diagram), Russian Revolution Dynasty slides + Bolshevik questions (Bolsheviks, Red/White Army, communism, Mensheviks, Lenin, Trotsky, Stolypin, Kerensky, 'pick a side'), Rule of Stalin textbook reading (totalitarianism), Edgenuity lessons”.

🧱 Unit 9: Cold War, China & India

After 1945: the Cold War between the USA (capitalism, democracy) and the USSR (communism), the Iron Curtain, NATO, the Berlin Airlift and Wall; Mao and communist China; Gandhi, independence and partition in India.
Goes with the class Canvas module: “Unit 9 After WWII: Cold War Personalities, Terms & Events slides + 1950s Terms & Personalities sheet (communism, capitalism, Truman, Marshall Plan, Iron Curtain, domino theory, containment, NATO, Warsaw Pact, Rosenbergs, Berlin Airlift, Mao, McCarthy), How did communism start in China video sheet, Reeducation under Mao (Five-Year Plans, Cultural Revolution, Red Guards), Indian Independence Review (Gandhi, partition, Nehru, Jinnah, Indira Gandhi)”.
