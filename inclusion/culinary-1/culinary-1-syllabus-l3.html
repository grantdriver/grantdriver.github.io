🌟 About this course

Culinary 1 builds skills in food preparation, nutrition and kitchen management.

Expect lots of hands-on learning: new cooking terms and techniques, and cooking labs.

All materials and ingredients are provided.

🗓️ What we do in class

🍳Cooking labs: mise en place, cooking and clean-up
🧽Everyone helps with classroom chores (dishes, laundry); this is a large part of the lab grade
📖Readings that follow the class lesson, closer to the regular text
📝Guided notes for each lesson
✅Quick checks at the end of a lesson or unit
🔊Read aloud on every notes page and reading

📚 Units, in order

Same units and same order as the class. Unit dates follow the class calendar.

🧼Unit 1
Safety & Sanitation
Hazards, germs, hand washing, gloves, the Danger Zone, cooking temperatures, storing, thawing and cleaning. Food Worker Card prep.
🥄Unit 2
Measuring & Function of Ingredients
Kitchen tools, measuring, what each baking ingredient does, the creaming method, cookie lab.
🧁Unit 3
Quick Breads
Quick vs. yeast breads, baking soda vs. powder, the muffin and biscuit methods.
🥨Unit 4
Yeast Breads
Flour, gluten, yeast, the stand mixer, pizza crust, pretzels, cinnamon rolls.
🥕Unit 5
Fruits & Vegetables
Fruit and vegetable groups, identification, cookware, stir-fry lab.
🔪Unit 6
Knife Skills
Knife parts and safety, basic cuts, blanching, the apple peeler.
🍲Unit 7
Roux & Mirepoix
Making a roux and a mirepoix; cream soups.
🍝Unit 8
Grains & Pasta
Carbohydrates, grain identification, rice and pasta, gluten experiment.
🥚Unit 9
Milk, Dairy & Eggs
Protein, milk processing, cheese, eggs 7 ways, omelets and German pancakes.
🍽️Unit 10
Table Setting & Etiquette
Setting a table (formal and informal), table manners.
📖Unit 11
Recipes Used in Class
All the class picture recipe cards in one book, plus each card on its own.
🌮Unit 12
Protein & Main Dishes
Tacos, chili and cornbread, chicken Caesar wraps, 2-day lasagna with breadsticks, fruit pizza, fridge cleanout, and finals cleaning day.

🎒 What you need

  • ✏️Pen/pencil and paper
  • 📁Binder or folder to hold handouts
  • 💻School-issued laptop (you will be told ahead when it is needed)
  • 🧑‍🍳Apron and hair tied back during cooking labs
  • 🪪WA Food Worker Card: the test is taken in class (see the card below)

⭐ How you are graded

  • 📊Grading is based on lab work, participation, cleaning, unit quizzes and a comprehensive final.
  • 👂Employability skills count too: listening, following directions, efficient use of time, appropriate language and workplace behavior.
  • 🏠Missed labs (excused) can be made up at home within 1 month for full credit and turned in on Canvas. Talk to the teacher if you cannot cook at home.
  • ⭐Up to 3 extra credit cooking labs, only if all required work is done.
  • 🤝If your IEP allows modified grading, your modified work counts for the same points as the class work.
Letter grades (class syllabus): A 93–100 · A− 90–92 · B+ 87–89 · B 83–86 · B− 80–82 · C+ 77–79 · C 73–76 · C− 70–72 · D+ 67–69 · D 60–66

💛 Classroom expectations

  • 💛Respect Others: appropriate language, manners and behavior.
  • 🙂Respect Yourself: take responsibility for your actions; full effort.
  • 🍴Respect Property: care for the classroom, equipment and tools.
  • 📵FHS cell phone policy: one warning, then the phone goes in a bag for the period.
  • 🚿Good personal hygiene for food safety: clean hair, body and clothes.

🦺 Kitchen safety

  • 🧼Wash hands before cooking; use gloves when told.
  • 🧑‍🍳Apron on and hair tied back for every cooking lab.
  • 🔪Knife safety: carry and use knives the way you are taught.
  • 🌡️Keep food out of the Danger Zone; cook to safe temperatures.
  • 🧽Clean and sanitize your station before you leave.
🪪 WA Food Worker Card
  • •You take the WA Food Worker Card test in class. The state card costs $10 and lasts 2 years.
  • •If you cannot pay with a card or afford the fee, see the teacher to make other arrangements.

🙋 Getting help

  • 🙋Ask for help early, before a test or due date
  • 🔊Read aloud and printables are on ferndaleinclusion.com
  • 📂If you are absent, get the notes and handouts when you come back
