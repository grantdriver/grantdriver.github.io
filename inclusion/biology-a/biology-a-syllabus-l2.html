🌟 What is this class?

Biology is the study of living things.

This is a lab science class.

We learn by looking, testing and talking together.

🗓️ What we do in class

📺Follow the class slides, made easier to read
📝Guided notes with a word bank
🔊Read aloud for notes, readings and quizzes
🃏Vocabulary cards for key words
🎮Review games before quizzes
✅Short quizzes with 3 choices
🔬Lab packets with a picture task card and safety card

📚 Units, in order

🧪Unit 0
Science Starts: Safety & Lab Skills
Learn lab safety and how to make a good guess (a hypothesis).
🐋Unit 1
Orcas & the Salish Sea
Learn about our orcas, the food web, and what makes orcas sick.
🌿Unit 2
Algae & Photosynthesis
Plants and algae use sun, water and air to make sugar and oxygen.
⛽Unit 3
Biofuel & Energy
Learn about energy and how algae oil can become fuel for cars.
🍎Unit 4
Food & Digestion
Follow food from your mouth to your stomach. Learn carbs, fats and proteins.
🫁Unit 5
Breathing, Exercise & Energy
Your cells burn food for energy. Exercise makes your heart and breathing faster.
🩸Unit 6
Blood Sugar & Diabetes
Insulin helps your body keep blood sugar just right. Learn about diabetes.
🦕Unit 7
Famous Fossils & Deep Time
Learn how fossils form and how rock layers show time.
🏁Unit 8
Final Review
Review every unit before the final.
