📜 Banquo and Macbeth: suspicion and fear — Shakespeare
BANQUO. Thou hast it now, King, Cawdor, Glamis, all,As the Weird Women promis’d; and, I fear,Thou play’dst most foully for’t;
MACBETH. O, full of scorpions is my mind, dear wife!Thou know’st that Banquo, and his Fleance, lives.
William Shakespeare, Macbeth, Act III, Scenes 1–2 (Project Gutenberg eBook #1533). Public domain.
Weird Women = the witches · foully = in an evil, unfair way · scorpions = stinging creatures; here, painful worries
