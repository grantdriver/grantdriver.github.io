🌟 About this course

Biology is a lab science course for 9th grade.

Students work together to study the natural world through observation, labs, readings, models and class discussion.

Semester 1 topics: ecology, cells, photosynthesis, respiration, homeostasis, the carbon cycle and evolution.

🗓️ What we do in class

📖Readings that follow the class lesson, closer to the regular text
📝Guided notes for each lesson
✅Quick checks at the end of a lesson or unit
🔊Read aloud on every notes page and reading
🎮Unit review games
👥Same units, same order and same points as the class
🔬Labs and projects with a prep and safety card, slides and a reflection

📚 Units, in order

Same units and same order as the class. Unit dates follow the class calendar.

🧪Unit 0
Science Starts: Safety & Lab Skills
Lab safety, observing, and If… then… because… hypotheses.
🐋Unit 1
Orcas & the Salish Sea
Southern Resident orcas, the Salish Sea food web, toxins that pile up, and ocean noise.
🌿Unit 2
Algae & Photosynthesis
How algae and plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make sugar and oxygen.
⛽Unit 3
Biofuel & Energy
Forms of energy, how algae oil becomes biodiesel, hot and cold reactions, renewable fuels, and the carbon cycle.
🍎Unit 4
Food & Digestion
Carbs, fats, and proteins, and how food travels from mouth to stomach to intestines.
🫁Unit 5
Breathing, Exercise & Energy
How cells burn food for energy, ATP energy coins, and what exercise does to heart rate, breathing, and CO2.
🩸Unit 6
Blood Sugar & Diabetes
Insulin is the key, the body keeps blood sugar just right, type 1 and type 2 diabetes, risk factors, and healthy choices.
🦕Unit 7
Famous Fossils & Deep Time
How fossils form, Washington fossils, rock layers, and the Twine Time timeline.
🏁Unit 8
Final Review
Picture review of the whole semester.

💛 Classroom expectations

  • ⏰Seated in your assigned spot on time; pick up handouts and your folder.
  • 📵Cell phone in your backpack.
  • 👀Check the screen for the daily lesson and do the warm-up.
  • 🙋Sign out one at a time for a restroom or water break, and get the teacher's attention first.
  • 🤝Respect the scientific community: everyone should feel safe to share ideas.
  • 🧹Turn in work, put away your folder, tidy up, and stay by your seat until the bell.

🦺 Lab safety

  • 🥽Wear goggles whenever chemicals, heat or glassware are used.
  • 🚫No food or beverages at lab stations; no sitting on lab stations.
  • 📋Stay at your assigned area and follow directions carefully.
  • 🧼Clean up and wash your hands after labs.
  • 🙋Report any spill, break or injury right away.

🙋 Getting help

  • 🙋Ask for help early, before a test or due date
  • 🔊Read aloud and printables are on ferndaleinclusion.com
  • 📂If you are absent, get the notes and handouts when you come back
