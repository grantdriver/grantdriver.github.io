Transitions L1 Hot Ham Cheese Shopping List (read aloud)

🛒 Shopping + Supply List · Hot Ham and Cheese

Sandwich

Transitions Level 1 · Cooking Lab · Thu 11/5/2026 · Blue 3rd period · For Grant and staff

⚠ Assumptions (please check)

• Class size: about 8 students + 3 staff = 11 sandwiches, plus 2 spares for drops or re-dos = 13

sandwiches. Change the totals if your count is different.

• Amounts: the Accessible Chef recipe lists only butter, bread, cheese, ham with no amounts. We use

2 bread, 1 cheese, 2 thin ham, a little butter per sandwich.

• Microwave: 30 seconds each. With 1 microwave, 13 turns take about 10 to 13 minutes, which fits the

25-minute cook block.

• Allergies / diet: check health plans and IEPs for dairy, gluten, or pork. Swaps: turkey for ham, gluten-

free bread, dairy-free cheese.

🧺 Food (for 13 sandwiches)

Item We need Buy

🍞 Bread (sandwich loaf) 26 slices 2 loaves (about 20 slices each)

🧀 Cheese slices (American or cheddar) 13 slices 1 pack of 16 to 24 slices

🍖 Deli ham, thin sliced 26 thin slices about 1 lb (or 2 packs, 8 to 9 oz each)

🧈 Butter, soft tub spread (easier to spread) about ¼ cup total 1 small tub (or 1 stick, left out 30 min to soften)
