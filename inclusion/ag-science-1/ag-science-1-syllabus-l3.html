🌟 About this course

Agricultural Science 1 explores farming and food, starting close to home in Whatcom County and Washington.

You will study crops, dairy and livestock, U.S. food and farm facts, FFA and ag careers.

You will also plan, do and present your own Ag Experience Project.

🗓️ What we do in class

📖Readings that follow the class lesson, closer to the regular text
📝Guided notes for each lesson
✅Quick checks at the end of a lesson or unit
🔊Read aloud on every notes page and reading
🎮Unit review games
🌱Labs and projects: task card, prep sheet, slides, reflection and a game

📚 Units, in order

Same units and same order as the class. Unit dates follow the class calendar.

🌾Unit 1
Ag Words & Whatcom Farms
Top ag words, what Whatcom County and Washington farms grow, local ag businesses, and the Tour de Whatcom field trip.
🥔Unit 2
Washington Crops: Potatoes, Wheat & Apples
Seed potatoes at a local farm, Washington wheat and barley, apples and Cosmic Crisp, plus the Potato and Apple labs.
🚜Unit 3
U.S. Food & Farm Facts
How much one farmer feeds, what we pay for food, farm families, exports, the future of farming, and the Ag Literacy poster.
🌱Unit 4
FFA & Ag Careers
What FFA is, the 3-circle model, colors, motto, emblem, the FFA Creed, contests, and ag careers.
📊Unit 5
Ag Experience Project
Plan, do, and share your own ag project: pick a topic, set a goal, keep a work log, make slides, and present.
🐄Unit 6
Dairy
Dairy cows, how milk is made and processed, dairy foods, cheese and yogurt, and making ice cream.
🐖Unit 7
Livestock Judging
Ideal sheep, goats, cattle, and pigs; muscle, structure, and balance; placing a class and giving reasons.
🍎Unit 8
Final Review
Review of every unit, plus the final work-log check.
🌍Unit 9
Ag Issueslater
Coming when the class gets there.
