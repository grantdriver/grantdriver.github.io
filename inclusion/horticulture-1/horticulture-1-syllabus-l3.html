🌟 About this course

Horticulture is the science and study of growing plants: fruits, vegetables, flowers and ornamental plants.

You will learn how plants live and grow, and propagate plants in the classroom greenhouse.

It prepares you for Horticulture 2, where students raise crops for the spring plant sale.

🗓️ What we do in class

📖Readings that follow the class lesson, closer to the regular text
📝Guided notes for each lesson
✅Quick checks at the end of a lesson or unit
🔊Read aloud on every notes page and reading
🎮Unit review games
🌱Greenhouse labs and projects
📒Ongoing: Horticulture Journal/notes, Plant ID Collection, Poinsettia Portfolio

📚 Units, in order

Same units and same order as the class. Unit dates follow the class calendar.

🏡Unit 0
Classroom & Greenhouse Basics
Class rules, greenhouse map and tools, safety, the Propagation Journal and leadership points.
🧑‍🌾Unit 1
Jobs in Horticulture
The branches of horticulture (fruit, flowers, vegetables, landscape) and the jobs people do.
🏷️Unit 2
Plant Names & Life Cycles
Scientific names (genus + species), flower types, and annual, biennial and perennial plants.
🌿Unit 3
Parts of the Plant
Roots, stems, leaves and flowers, photosynthesis, and how bees pollinate.
🔎Unit 4
Plant ID
Learn to name 16 greenhouse bedding plants and fill in a Plant ID card.
🪱Unit 5
Soil & Nutrients
What plants need to grow: light, water, air, soil, and the nutrients N, P and K.
🌰Unit 6
Seeds
Parts of a seed, how seeds sprout, and sowing seeds in the greenhouse.
✂️Unit 7
Propagation
Making new plants from seeds, cuttings and division.
🌺Unit 8
Poinsettias
Growing and caring for poinsettias, bracts and short days, pests, and the poinsettia sale.
💐Unit 9
Wreaths & Floral Design
Making wreaths, centerpieces and succulent planters, and getting ready for the plant sale.
