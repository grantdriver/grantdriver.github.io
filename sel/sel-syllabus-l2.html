🌟 What is this class?

SEL means social emotional learning.

You learn about feelings, calm-down tools and goals.

You practice skills with friends and in real life.

🗓️ What we do in class

😊Check-in and energy check (1 to 5)
🔁Last Time review
⭐Main lesson: slides, choice cards and practice
🃏Card sorts with a partner
🎮Review games
📒Unit notebook or project page
👋Closing: exit line and a preview

📚 Units, in order

🤝Unit 1
Foundations & Community
Meet the class, learn our routines and check-ins.
🪞Unit 2
Self-Awareness: Who Am I?
Name your feelings and your strengths. Make an identity map.
🧰Unit 3
Self-Management: My Calm-Down Toolkit
Learn calm-down tools and make a calm-down goal.
🕵️Unit 4
Social Awareness: Feelings Detective
Read face, body and voice clues. Learn empathy and ways to help.
💬Unit 5
Relationship Skills
Start a talk, listen, take turns and use kind words.
🛑Unit 6
Stop, Think, Choose
Stop and think before you choose. Think about what happens next.
🌱Unit 7
I Can Grow
Your interests, what matters to you, and a growth mindset.
🎯Unit 8
My Game Plan: Goals & Routines
Set a goal, make a plan and track it.
👀Unit 9
Reading the Room
Notice what a place needs: quiet, voice level and space.
🧩Unit 10
Team Up & Work It Out
Work as a team and solve problems fairly.
🏆Unit 11
My SEL Showcase
Show your best work from the year.

🎒 What you need

  • 📁Folder
  • 📒Unit notebook
  • ✏️Pencil

⭐ How you show what you learned

  • 🙋Take part in class each day.
  • 📒Finish your notebook or binder page.
  • 🗣️Share your ideas: talk, write, point or use your device.
  • 🏆Show your unit project at the end.

💛 How we act in class

  • 💛Be kind to everyone.
  • 🏫Keep our room safe.
  • 👂Listen when someone shares.
  • 🙋You can pass, take a break or ask for help.

🙋 Getting help

  • 🙋Ask a teacher or para when you are stuck
  • 🔊Use Read aloud on the class page
  • 📂Missed a day? Your work is on the class page
