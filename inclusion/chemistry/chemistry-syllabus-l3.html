🌟 About this course

Chemistry is a lab science course for 10th grade (and some 11th and 12th graders).

Students investigate how matter and energy interact, using the OpenSciEd curriculum. Each unit is built around a real-world phenomenon.

It also helps prepare you for the state science test (WCAS) in 11th grade.

🗓️ What we do in class

📖Readings that follow the class lesson, closer to the regular text
📝Guided notes for each lesson
✅Quick checks at the end of a lesson or unit
🔊Read aloud on every notes page and reading
🎮Unit review games
👥Same units, same order and same points as the class
🔬Labs at lab stations, then a class debrief (data and a claim)

📚 Units, in order

Same units and same order as the class. Unit dates follow the class calendar.

🥽Unit 0
Lab Safety & Equipment
Goggles on, no food, tell the teacher, yell “Code one” if someone is hurt. Lab tools: beaker, graduated cylinder, balance, test tube. Hazard symbols.
⚡Unit 1A
Lightning 1: Charges & Static
Atoms have protons (+), neutrons and electrons (−). Rubbing moves electrons, and that makes a static charge.
🧲Unit 1B
Lightning 2: Electric Forces
Opposite charges pull, like charges push. More charge and less distance make a stronger force. A charged balloon can pull on a neutral object.
🌩️Unit 1C
Lightning 3: The Strike & Staying Safe
Charge builds in a storm cloud, then electrons jump to the ground. Metal and salt water are conductors. How to stay safe in a storm.
🪐Unit 2A
Space 1: Atoms & the Periodic Table
What people need to live off Earth. Elements are the building blocks. Element cards and the periodic table: protons, electrons, shells, valence electrons, and trends in atom size and electronegativity.
🌈Unit 2B
Space 2: Spectra, Bonding & Water
How scientists find water and elements far away (spectra), flame colors, polar and nonpolar bonds, the four bonding models, and the special properties of water.
⚗️Unit 2C
Space 3: Formulas & Reactions
Making fertilizer in space (ammonia vs ammonium), Lewis structures, writing chemical formulas, balancing equations so atoms are conserved, and types of chemical reactions.
🦪Unit 3
Oysterslater
Semester 2: why oysters are dying and how chemistry can help (acids, bases and pH). Details coming when the class gets there.
🧊Unit 4
Polar Icelater
Coming later. Details will be added when the class gets there.
🔋Unit 5
Energylater
Semester 2: energy from chemical and nuclear reactions. Details coming later.

💛 Classroom expectations

  • ✅Be Ready: charged laptop, pencil or pen, lab book, phone in backpack, on time and in your assigned seat.
  • 🤲Be Responsible: pick up handouts, do your lab job, stay on task and at your station, clean up.
  • 💛Be Respectful: listen when anyone is sharing, raise your hand, take turns, care for equipment, no pranks.
  • 🔔Pack up only when told, and stay in class until the bell.

🦺 Lab safety

  • 🥽Goggles any time chemicals, heat or glassware are used. No exceptions. Lab aprons are provided.
  • 🚫No food, drinks or gum in the lab; never use lab glassware for food.
  • 👅Treat all chemicals as dangerous: do not touch, taste or smell unless instructed.
  • 🗣️If you or your partner are hurt, yell “Code one, Code one.” Eye splash: eyewash for 20 minutes.
  • 💇Tie back long hair; secure jewelry and loose clothing; shoes must cover the whole foot.
  • 📝You sign the Science Safety Contract.
