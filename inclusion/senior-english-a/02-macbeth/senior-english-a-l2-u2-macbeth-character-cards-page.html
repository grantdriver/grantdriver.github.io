🎭 Who's Who in Macbeth

Senior English A · Unit 2 · Level 1 Advanced · character cards · cut apart · use for the character match and poster
Name: ______________________________
🗡️ Macbeth
A brave lord who wants to be king.
👸 Lady Macbeth
His wife. She pushes him. Later she feels guilty.
🛡️ Banquo
Macbeth's friend. His sons will be kings.
👑 Duncan
The good, kind king.
🦁 Macduff
He fights Macbeth and wins.
🔮 The Witches
Three strange sisters. They tell the future.
🤴 Malcolm
Duncan's son. The new king at the end.
