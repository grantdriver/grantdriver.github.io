🍕 Microwave Pizza Bowl

You will need

  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 1/8 tsp baking powder
  • 1/16 tsp baking soda
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp pizza sauce
  • 1 tbsp mozzarella cheese
  • pepperoni
  • 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning

Tools

bowl (card says mug), measuring spoons, spoon, spatula, microwave, oven mitts

Steps

  1. In the bowl: add flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. Add milk and olive oil. Mix together. Spread the sauce on top. Sprinkle on the cheese.
  3. Sprinkle on pepperoni and Italian herbs. Microwave for 1 min 15 sec (Grant's time). Careful, it's hot!
