📝 Notes (fill in the blanks)

1. 🔣 A   is an object, image or action that stands for a bigger idea.
2. 🆓 Free verse is poetry with no set rhyme or rhythm.
3. ↩️ A   is where the poet chooses to end a line.
4. ➡️ Enjambment is when a sentence keeps going onto the next line.
5. 🔄 A   is a change in tone or idea in a poem.

📜 The Old Sneakers — our team
They wait by the door,laces loose like they are tired of running.They have been to every game,every rainy walk home,every first day.They are too small now.I keep them anyway.
Original example poem written for this class site (stand-in for a copyrighted poem used in class). In class: “Perhaps the World Ends Here” by Joy Harjo (title only, copyrighted).

🔎 Analyze

1. What bigger idea do the sneakers stand for?
2. Where is there a shift in the poem? What changes?
