💗 Part 1: Pretend genetic risk (a model, not a medical test)

Do not use a real family health survey unless a student chooses to. Flip a coin twice for each of 6 “genes.”

A higher score means a higher modeled risk, not a diagnosis. Genes in this model do not change when

the environment changes.
