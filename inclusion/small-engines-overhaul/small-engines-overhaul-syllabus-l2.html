🌟 What is this class?

Small Engines is a shop class.

You learn tools, engine parts and how engines work.

Then you take apart and rebuild a small engine.

🗓️ What we do in class

📺Follow the class slides, made easier to read
📝Guided notes with a word bank
🔊Read aloud for notes, readings and quizzes
🃏Vocabulary cards for key words
🎮Review games before quizzes
🔧Shop labs with a picture step checklist

📚 Units, in order

🛡️Unit 0
Shop Start-Up & Safety
Shop rules, safety gear, fuel and fire safety, and the safety test.
📏Unit 1
Measuring Tools
Rulers, calipers and gauges, and how to read them.
🧑‍🔧Unit 2
Engine Careers
Jobs that fix engines and the skills bosses want.
⚙️Unit 3
4-Stroke Theory & Engine Parts
The four strokes and the main engine parts.
💨Unit 4
Compression
Why an engine needs compression, and the compression test.
⛽Unit 5
Carburetion & Fuel
How the carburetor mixes air and fuel. Use fresh fuel.
⚡Unit 6
Ignition
How the engine makes a spark, and the spark plug gap.
📘Unit 7
Using the Repair Manual
Find the model number and look up parts in the manual.
🔧Unit 8
Engine Teardown & Rebuild
Take an engine apart and put it back together.

🎒 What you need

  • 🥽Safety glasses
  • 👖Coveralls
  • 🥾Closed-toe work shoes
  • ✏️Pencil for lab sheets

⭐ How you are graded

  • ⭐Earn 20 points each day for being ready, working and cleaning up.
  • 🔧Your engine project earns points too.
  • 🧾Finish all class work and pass the safety test before you get an engine.
  • 🤝Your IEP team helps decide how your work is graded.

💛 How we act in class

  • ✅Come prepared and ready to work.
  • 💪Work the entire period.
  • 🙂Keep a positive attitude.
  • 🧰Put all tools away properly.
  • 🧽Clean your area before you leave.

🦺 Shop safety

  • 🥽Wear safety glasses in the shop.
  • 🧰Use tools the way you are shown.
  • 💇No loose clothes, hair or jewelry near engines.
  • ⛽Fuel catches fire. Follow fuel and fire rules.
  • 🧾Pass the shop safety test first.

🙋 Getting help

  • 🙋Ask a teacher or para when you are stuck
  • 🔊Use Read aloud on the class page
  • 📂Missed a day? Your work is on the class page
