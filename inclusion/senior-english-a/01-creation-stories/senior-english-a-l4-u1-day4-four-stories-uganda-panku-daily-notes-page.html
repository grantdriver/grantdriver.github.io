📜 Chinese: P’an Ku
In this Chinese story, the universe begins as a huge egg holding chaos.P’an Ku grows inside, breaks it open, and pushes sky and earth apart for 18,000 years.When he dies, his body becomes the world: breath becomes wind, eyes the sun and moon, body the mountains and rivers.
Brief summary written by our team of a traditional Chinese story (P’an Ku / Pangu). Versions differ.
