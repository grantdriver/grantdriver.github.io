🔍 Day 2: How to Read a Myth: Genesis

📜 “In the beginning” — Genesis (KJV)
In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.
💬 In plain words: First there is darkness and water. Then God speaks, and light appears.
Genesis 1:1–3, King James Version (Project Gutenberg eBook #8001). Public domain.
void = empty · the deep = deep waters · without form = no shape

🧙 Day 6: Recurring Roles & Meaningful Symbols

📜 The first human and a job to do — Genesis (KJV)
And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.
💬 In plain words: God makes the first man from dust and gives him a job: take care of the garden.
Genesis 2:7, 2:15, King James Version (Project Gutenberg eBook #8001). Public domain.
formed = shaped · dress it = tend it, work it · keep it = take care of it

🌈 Day 11: Noah and the Flood

📜 The flood and the promise — Genesis (KJV)
And the rain was upon the earth forty days and forty nights.
I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth.
💬 In plain words: Build a boat. It rained 40 days. A dove brought a leaf, so the water was going down. The rainbow is a promise.
Genesis 6:14, 7:12, 8:11, 9:13, King James Version (Project Gutenberg eBook #8001). Public domain.
ark = a large boat · pitch = tar to keep out water · abated = gone down · token = sign · covenant = a serious promise
