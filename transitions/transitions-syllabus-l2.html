🌟 What is this class?

Transitions is about life after high school.

You learn to speak up, live on your own and work.

You practice real skills, step by step.

🗓️ What we do in class

☀️Warm-up check-in
🔁Last Time review: put steps in order
📣Lesson with step cards
🤝Partner or station practice
🙌Solo tries
🗂️Wrap-up: share and exit check
🍳Some Fridays: cooking lab or outing

📚 Units, in order

🗣️Unit 1
Self-Advocacy
Know your strengths and needs, and ask for help.
🏠Unit 2
Independent Living
Wash dishes, wipe tables, sort, sweep and fold laundry.
🧼Unit 3
Health, Wellness & Relationships
Hygiene routines, healthy choices and personal space.
💼Unit 4
Employment & Career Exploration
Job skills, your interests and finishing task cards.
💵Unit 5
Financial Literacy & Money
Coins and bills, prices, needs and wants, and paying.
🚌Unit 6
Community Navigation & Mobility
Walk safely, read signs and follow a route.

🎒 What you need

  • 📒Binder
  • ✏️Pencil
  • 🃏Skill Card

⭐ How you show what you learned

  • 🙋Take part in class each day.
  • 📒Finish your notebook or binder page.
  • 🗣️Share your ideas: talk, write, point or use your device.
  • 🏆Show your unit project at the end.

💛 How we act in class

  • 💛Be kind and patient with partners.
  • 🏫Be safe at every station.
  • 🟨Use the “I need help” card.
  • 🧹Reset and clean up your station.

🦺 Kitchen & cleaning safety

  • 🧼Wash your hands before cooking.
  • 🧴Read the label. Use cleaners only with an adult.
  • 🔥Stoves and pans get hot.
  • 🔪Knives are sharp. Use them as shown.

🙋 Getting help

  • 🙋Ask a teacher or para when you are stuck
  • 🔊Use Read aloud on the class page
  • 📂Missed a day? Your work is on the class page
