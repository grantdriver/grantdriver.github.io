🌟 About this course

US History is a required survey course for 10th grade.

Goal: leave with a basic understanding of how American history unfolded, its major events and people, and how they affect your life.

Each class has a daily goal, reviewed at the start and end of the period.

🗓️ What we do in class

📖Readings that follow the class lesson, closer to the regular text
📝Guided notes for each lesson
✅Quick checks at the end of a lesson or unit
🔊Read aloud on every notes page and reading
🎮Unit review games
👥Same units, same order and same points as the class
🎨Mini-projects in each unit: research, art and presentations

📚 Units, in order

Same units and same order as the class. Unit dates follow the class calendar.

🗓️Unit 1
Timeline & Political Cartoons
Big events from 1865 to today in order. How to read a political cartoon with BASIC.
🦬Unit 2
The Heartland: Moving West
Manifest Destiny, the railroad, homesteaders and sod houses, cowboys, the buffalo, and what westward growth did to Native nations.
🗽Unit 3
Immigration & Cities
Why people came, Ellis Island and Angel Island, the Chinese Exclusion Act, crowded city tenements.
🏭Unit 4
Industry & the Progressives
Inventors and big business, Boss Tweed, muckrakers, child labor, The Jungle and safe food laws, Jane Addams.
⚖️Unit 5
Civil Rights at the Turn of the Century
Jim Crow laws, Plessy v. Ferguson, Ida B. Wells, Booker T. Washington and W.E.B. Du Bois.
🌎Unit 6
Imperialism
Why the US expanded, the Spanish-American War, Hawaii, the Panama Canal, the Big Stick.
🪖Unit 7
World War I
MAIN causes, why the US joined, trenches and new weapons, the home front, the 14 Points and the Treaty of Versailles.
🗳️Unit 8
Women's Suffrage
Susan B. Anthony, NAWSA and the NWP, Alice Paul and the Silent Sentinels, the 19th Amendment (1920).
🎷Unit 9
The 1920s & Harlem Renaissance
The Great Migration, the Harlem Renaissance and jazz, Prohibition and gangsters, flappers, the Scopes Trial.
🍞Unit 10
The Great Depression & New Deal
The 1929 crash, Hoovervilles, the Dust Bowl, FDR and the New Deal.
