7️⃣ Seven Types of Creation Stories

Senior English A · Unit 1 · Level 1 Basic · picture cards · keep in your folder
Name: ______________________________
🌪️ Creation from chaos
A mess becomes order.
🦫 Earth diver
An animal dives for mud.
🧍 World parent
Sky and earth parents, or a body becomes the world.
🥚 Cosmic egg
The world starts in an egg.
🗣️ Ex nihilo
Made from nothing, often by a word.
🪜
🪜 Emergence
People climb up through worlds.
🧩
🧩 Combination
A story can fit two types.
