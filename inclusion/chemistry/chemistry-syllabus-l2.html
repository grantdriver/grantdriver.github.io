🌟 What is this class?

Chemistry is the study of matter and energy.

It is a lab science class.

Each unit starts with a real-world question, like how lightning works.

🗓️ What we do in class

📺Follow the class slides, made easier to read
📝Guided notes with a word bank
🔊Read aloud for notes, readings and quizzes
🃏Vocabulary cards for key words
🎮Review games before quizzes
✅Short quizzes with 3 choices
🔬Lab packets with picture steps

📚 Units, in order

🥽Unit 0
Lab Safety & Equipment
Learn the lab rules, the lab tools and the hazard signs.
⚡Unit 1A
Lightning 1: Charges & Static
Atoms have plus and minus parts. Rubbing moves electrons and makes static.
🧲Unit 1B
Lightning 2: Electric Forces
Opposite charges pull. Same charges push. Closer means stronger.
🌩️Unit 1C
Lightning 3: The Strike & Staying Safe
Learn how lightning strikes and how to stay safe in a storm.
🪐Unit 2A
Space 1: Atoms & the Periodic Table
What people need to live in space. Elements and the periodic table.
🌈Unit 2B
Space 2: Spectra, Bonding & Water
How scientists find water far away. Flame colors and how atoms join.
⚗️Unit 2C
Space 3: Formulas & Reactions
Write chemical formulas and balance equations. Atoms are never lost.

🎒 What you need

  • 📁Science folder (given to you)
  • ✏️Pen or pencil
  • 📓Lab book
  • 💻Charged laptop

⭐ How you are graded

  • 📓Half of your grade is your lab book: warm-ups, lessons, labs.
  • ✅Half is tests and quizzes.
  • 🔁You can retake test parts as many times as you need.
  • ⏰Late work is accepted. Old work is in the Extra Assignments bin.
  • 🤝Your IEP team helps decide how your work is graded.

💛 How we act in class

  • ✅Be ready: laptop, pencil, lab book, on time.
  • 🤲Be responsible: do your lab job and stay at your station.
  • 💛Be respectful: listen, take turns, work together.
  • 📵Cell phone in your backpack.

🦺 Lab safety

  • 🥽Wear goggles with chemicals, heat or glass.
  • 🚫No food, drinks or gum in the lab.
  • 👅Do not touch, taste or smell chemicals unless told.
  • 🗣️If someone is hurt, yell “Code one, Code one.”
  • 💇Tie back long hair. Wear closed shoes.
  • 🙋Tell the teacher about any spill, break or cut.

🙋 Getting help

  • 🙋Ask a teacher or para when you are stuck
  • 🔊Use Read aloud on the class page
  • 📂Missed a day? Your work is on the class page
