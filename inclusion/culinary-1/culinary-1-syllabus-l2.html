🌟 What is this class?

Culinary is a cooking class.

You learn kitchen safety, cooking words and skills.

You cook lots of tasty food in labs.

🗓️ What we do in class

🍳Cooking labs with picture recipe cards
🧽Class chores: dishes, folding laundry
📺Follow the class slides, made easier to read
📝Guided notes with a word bank
🔊Read aloud for notes, readings and quizzes
🃏Vocabulary cards for key words

📚 Units, in order

🧼Unit 1
Safety & Sanitation
Germs, hand washing, gloves and safe food temperatures.
🥄Unit 2
Measuring & Function of Ingredients
Kitchen tools, measuring, and what each baking ingredient does.
🧁Unit 3
Quick Breads
Quick breads use baking soda or powder. Make muffins and biscuits.
🥨Unit 4
Yeast Breads
Yeast makes dough rise. Make pizza crust, pretzels and rolls.
🥕Unit 5
Fruits & Vegetables
Name fruits and vegetables. Cook a stir-fry.
🔪Unit 6
Knife Skills
Knife parts, knife safety and basic cuts.
🍲Unit 7
Roux & Mirepoix
Make a roux and a mirepoix to start a cream soup.
🍝Unit 8
Grains & Pasta
Grains, rice and pasta, and a gluten experiment.
🥚Unit 9
Milk, Dairy & Eggs
Milk, cheese and eggs. Cook eggs many ways.
🍽️Unit 10
Table Setting & Etiquette
Set a table and use good table manners.
📖Unit 11
Recipes Used in Class
All the class recipes on picture recipe cards.
🌮Unit 12
Protein & Main Dishes
Cook main dishes like tacos and lasagna. Clean up for finals.

🎒 What you need

  • ✏️Pen or pencil and paper
  • 📁Binder or folder for handouts
  • 💻School laptop on days you are told
  • 🧑‍🍳Apron and hair tied back for labs

⭐ How you are graded

  • 🍳Lab work, cleaning and class chores
  • ✅Unit quizzes and a final
  • 👂Job skills: listening, following directions, using time well
  • 🏠Missed a lab? You can make it up at home within 1 month
  • 🤝Your IEP team helps decide how your work is graded.

💛 How we act in class

  • 💛Respect others: kind words and manners.
  • 🙂Respect yourself: try your best.
  • 🍴Respect property: care for tools and equipment.
  • 📵No cell phones.
  • ⏰Come on time and ready to cook.

🦺 Kitchen safety

  • 🧼Wash hands before you cook.
  • 🧑‍🍳Wear an apron. Tie back your hair.
  • 🔪Knives are sharp. Use them the way you are shown.
  • 🔥Stoves and pans get hot. Give them space.
  • 🧽Clean up your station.
🪪 Food Worker Card
  • •You take the Washington Food Worker Card test in class.
  • •The card costs $10. Talk to the teacher if that is a problem.

🙋 Getting help

  • 🙋Ask a teacher or para when you are stuck
  • 🔊Use Read aloud on the class page
  • 📂Missed a day? Your work is on the class page
