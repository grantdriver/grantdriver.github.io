🌟 What is this class?

Health helps you make healthy choices for life.

You learn about your body, feelings, food and safety.

You practice making good decisions.

🗓️ What we do in class

📺Follow the class slides, made easier to read
📝Guided notes with a word bank
🔊Read aloud for notes, readings and quizzes
🃏Vocabulary cards for key words
🎮Review games before quizzes
✅Short quizzes with 3 choices

📚 Units, in order

🌈Unit 1
Wellness
Wellness, sleep, stress, making decisions and SMART goals.
🥗Unit 2
Food & Nutrition
The Healthy Eating Plate, food labels and added sugar.
🧠Unit 3
Substance Use & Abuse
How drugs and alcohol affect the teen brain, and how to get help.
💬Unit 4
Social-Emotional Health
Name feelings, set boundaries and handle stress and bullying.
🫶Unit 5A
Sexual Health 1: Bodies, Pregnancy & Relationships
How bodies grow, and healthy and safe relationships, including consent.
🩺Unit 5B
Sexual Health 2: STIs, Contraception & Safer Sex
How to prevent infections and stay healthy, and where to get tested.
🚑Unit 6
Safety, First Aid & CPR
When to call 911, basic first aid and hands-only CPR.
🌟Unit 7
Hope, Purpose & Wonder
Hope, goals and your purpose. Look back at the semester.
